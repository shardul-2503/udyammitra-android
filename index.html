PRACTICAL SKILLS

Quick business learning

Know your full cost

Include raw materials, labour, packaging, transport, market fees and wastage before setting a price.

Start with a small batch

Use actual orders and recent sales to adjust production. Avoid making more than you can safely store or sell.

Keep payment records

Write down the amount, date, buyer and payment status. Never share OTPs or banking passwords with buyers.
